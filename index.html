Reading this calendar

  • ★ marks anything sat in person — IRAT/TRATs, in-class quizzes, exams and OSCEs.
  • Everything unstarred is submitted on CanvasMed: written assignments, homework and survey submissions as well as quizzes. Tick those off as you submit them — the starred sittings have no tick box.
  • A dotted box marks the day a quiz opens; the solid box is the deadline.
  • The Canvas ↗ link opens the assignment itself where CanvasMed has published one; where it hasn't yet, the greyed link opens that course's assignment list instead.

Class schedule tab

  • Pick your small-group college at the top. Mandatory sittings are underlined, flipped and team sessions are bold.
  • Hover a class for its faculty, prep material and learning objectives; click it to expand the block in place.
  • School events layers on clubs, service, wellbeing and career sessions in amber. Star the ones you want on their card, then switch to ★ Saved for only those, or None for a clean timetable.
  • + My event adds your own to that week — separate from the + My deadline items on the assignments calendar.
